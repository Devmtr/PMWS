About Us

A leading industrial landmark in the heart of Abu Dhabi

About Us

In the heart of Abu Dhabi, from Musaffah, Pure Meat Industries stands as a leading strategic industrial landmark, specializing with exacting precision and high professionalism in the processing of animal slaughter by-products through a smart, innovative operating system.

We do far more than supply food products; we build bridges of trust. We apply the finest internationally recognized food safety standards, fully integrated with the guidance and oversight of the regulatory authorities of the United Arab Emirates.

In doing so, we meet the aspirations of local and regional markets with the highest standards of reliability and purity, and we are the partner that hotel kitchens, catering companies and supermarkets can rely on.

Our Vision

To stand at the summit of regional and local leadership as the first and most trusted industrial partner in the supply and processing of slaughter by-products and processed meat products, upholding the highest standards of purity and safety.

Our Mission

To deliver impeccably hygienic food products, governed by the most rigorous Critical Control Points (CCPs), supporting the food security and hospitality sectors with the highest standards of sustainable quality.

Our Core Values

01

Integrity and Safety First

Consumer health is our absolute priority. We apply every health requirement with uncompromising precision.

02

Technical Innovation

We keep pace with the latest advanced, automated machinery and equipment in the food sector, and put it to work for quality and efficiency.

03

Quality

A continuous pursuit of visual and microscopic clarity and purity in every finished product.

04

We operate hand in hand with environmental and health regulations to ensure sustainable, clean processes.
