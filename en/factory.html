Operational Engineering: The Product's Journey Through the Plant

Our facility is built on a unique spatial layout and operational design that ensures complete segregation of workflows, separating the receiving and primary processing area from the clean area, to prevent any cross-contamination. The process flows through the following stages:

01
CCP1

Precise Physical Intake

Raw materials are received from certified slaughterhouses and undergo immediate visual and physical inspection; any item that does not meet specifications is rejected. Accepted materials go straight into temperature-controlled cold storage at 1 to 4°C.

02
CCP2

Initial Soaking and Automated Thermal Scalding

Materials are soaked in purified water, then fed into advanced scalding machines that use precision steam to maintain temperatures between 60 and 65°C for a specific duration, effectively softening and loosening hair follicles.

03
CCP3

Precision Scraping and Safe Flame Treatment

Hair is removed mechanically with sharp blades, followed by a rapid pass through LPG-fueled flame ovens lasting no more than 30 seconds, eliminating fine hairs and facilitating the efficient removal of hooves and nails.

04

Final Cleaning and Smart Sanitization

Products are washed in specialized machinery using food-grade vinegar solution (2% concentration) or coarse salt (3% concentration) together with specialized stiff brushes, removing traces of singeing and restoring the product's brilliant, pristine white appearance.

05
CCP4

Strategic Cold Storage

Products are stored in sanitary containers within cold rooms at 1 to 4°C for a minimum of 24 hours, ensuring tissue stabilization before the dispatch and packaging stage.

06

Cold Chain, Transport and Distribution

Products are shipped by a fleet of officially licensed refrigerated vehicles that maintain optimal temperatures until delivery to our customers in the catering and commercial sectors.
