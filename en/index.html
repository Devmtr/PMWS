Why Pure Meat?

Complete Workflow Segregation

A unique spatial layout and operational design that separates the receiving and primary processing area from the clean area, preventing any cross-contamination.

Four Critical Control Points

CCP1 to CCP4 govern intake, thermal scalding, scraping and flame treatment, and cold storage.

Stainless Steel Equipment

The latest automated machinery, built from 400-series stainless steel, delivering maximum production capacity with minimal waste.

Pristine Products

Brilliant white trotters free of hair and hooves, heads free of impurities and odors, and tripe cleared of all residues.

An Unbroken Cold Chain

From cold storage at intake, through cold rooms, to delivery in licensed refrigerated vehicles.

Regulatory Compliance

Full coordination with, and adherence to, the technical requirements of the Abu Dhabi Agriculture and Food Safety Authority and the Environment Agency – Abu Dhabi.
